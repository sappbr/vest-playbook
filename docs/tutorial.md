# Tutorial: one account

This teaches the loop. The goal is one credited claim, not ten accounts.

The pass odds on this account are 3 / (3 + 10) = 23.1% if you treat the daily loss as the stop and the target as the take profit. Expected cost to pass is $864.50. With $200 you get one attempt. That is why the first account is the whole path until it pays.

After the $1,200 credit, the reserve rule is the lesson. Four fees stay untouched. The next purchase comes from the surplus.
